Middle East · Riyadh

Drone hits Riyadh’s tallest tower again

Houthi fighters struck the 385 m PIF Tower — home of the Saudi sovereign wealth fund — for the second time in under a week. Eleven were hurt: four Egyptians, three Nepalis, two Indians and two Saudis. The tower was first hit on 19 Nov, with no injuries; the coalition vowed a decisive response.

United States · Brooklyn

Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts

A Brooklyn jury convicted Huawei of bank fraud, wire fraud, Iran sanctions violations, trade-secret theft conspiracy and money laundering. It cleared the firm of racketeering and obstruction. The 11-week trial heard 60+ prosecution witnesses in a case opened in 2018. Sentencing is set for 14 September 2027. Huawei will appeal.

Space · Jiuquan, China

Shenzhou-24 lifts off with Pakistan’s first astronaut

Shenzhou-24 launched at 00:18:52 UTC from Jiuquan on a Long March 2F, carrying Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40. Ali is the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut aboard China’s Tiangong station. The crew will stay 5–10 days to run experiments.

Markets · New York

Dow tops 50,000 as Nvidia slides

The Dow closed at 50,018.70, up 423.39 (+0.85%). The S&P 500 fell 0.50% to 7,201.09 and the Nasdaq 1.48% to 23,733.54. Nvidia dropped 5.86% to $155.38 a day after jumping 31%. Dell revenue hit $54.94bn, up 103% on the year, on an AI server backlog of $132.3bn.

Ukraine · Gulf of Finland

Zelenskyy admits Ukraine hit Russian ship

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed Ukraine’s drone strike on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II, saying it carried weapons and was a legitimate target. The ship caught fire, drifted into Finnish waters and was towed to St Petersburg on Sunday. Norway urged caution.

China · Shanghai

Shanghai party chief under probe

Chen Jining, 62, Shanghai party chief and Politburo member, is under investigation for suspected serious discipline and law violations. He is the fourth Politburo member probed since 2025 and the first Shanghai chief probed since 2006. On Wednesday Shanghai’s leadership pledged loyalty to Beijing.

United States · Cyber

CIA and ODNI hit by cyberattack

CNN reported on 25 Nov that the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence appear to have been hit by a cyberattack touching unclassified information. It is the latest in a run of US government and private-sector breaches, and is not linked to the FBI database attack.

France · Paris

Sarkozy and Bruni ordered to trial

French officials ordered the corruption case against former president Nicolas Sarkozy and his wife Carla Bruni to go to trial. The first pre-trial hearing is set for 1 April 2027.

United States · Washington

Trump gives Giuliani the Medal of Freedom

President Donald Trump awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom — the highest US civilian honour — to former New York mayor Rudy Giuliani, 82, in a closed Oval Office ceremony. He is the third recipient of Trump’s second term. Giuliani was fined $148m in 2023 for defaming two Georgia election workers.

An Israeli air strike in western Gaza City killed Naeem Abu Nahel, 50, Hamas’s internal security director. Seven people were killed on Tuesday, including two police officers. UNRWA worker Adel al-Helo, a 35-year veteran, died in Jabalia camp.

Poland plans to summon Russia’s ambassador after three Russian missiles violated its airspace. On Wednesday a Polish official warned that expelling the envoy could risk an “outbreak of war”.

12 · Houthis 60 km from Aden, army says

Yemen’s military said Houthi forces have advanced to within 60 km of Aden, the temporary capital of the internationally recognised government, but will not take the city.

13 · Landmine toll highest in five years

Landmines and explosive remnants killed or injured at least 6,929 people in 2025, up 10% and the highest since 2020. Myanmar had the most victims, 1,891; Syria had 1,602, up more than 50%. 84% of victims were civilians, half of them children.

14 · WHO pulls Ebola team from Congo hospital

WHO chief Tedros said health workers were evacuated from a hospital in Akwe, Ituri, after sheltering three days from heavy fighting. The DRC outbreak has killed 5,775 of 11,762 cases — the worst recorded in the country.

Zelenskyy said Ukraine struck two Russian ships off Libya: the corvette Merkury sank with a Ka-27 helicopter, and the cargo ship Angara was also hit. The strikes took place on 21–22 Nov.
